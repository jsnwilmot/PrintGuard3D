## Meaning and possible over-reading of Manufacturer Verified

The published interval/text and exact stored definition may be source-matched without every linked video, model revision, usage interpretation or physical service procedure being completely verified. The existing C5/Pro definitions already disclose unreviewed video precautions and separate unsupported references; this audit does not remove those safeguards or grant broader verification.

A badge would be misleading if interpreted as exhaustive manufacturer approval of the whole catalogue, every Pro transfer, a resolved T=5/T=10 decision, a guaranteed operating-hour substitution, an arbitrary aftermarket kit, or fully audited servicing instructions. There is no evidence for those broader interpretations. The app's manually entered operating-hour field is not independent proof of the manufacturer's intended printing/component counter; no real-user readings were examined.

F07 remains PARTIAL. No dangerous contradiction in active **fixed numeric intervals** was identified, but unresolved semantics and incomplete safety coverage are P1 source limitations for Architect review. The existing Pro rule is narrow; a manufacturer video showing a Pro is not evidence that every series rule applies to Pro. No current application badge is reclassified in this branch.

3. Architect/owner explicitly approve the proposed verified scope and any source-disclosure changes. This evidence PR alone is insufficient authorization.
