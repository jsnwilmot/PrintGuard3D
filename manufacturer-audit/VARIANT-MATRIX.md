F bands: daily working-hours T < 5 -> 9 months; 5 < T < 10 -> 6 months; T > 10 -> 3 months. Exactly 5 and 10 are unassigned. XY bands: daily printing T < 1 -> 3 months; 1-5 -> 2 months; T >= 5 -> 1 month. Z bands: T < 1 -> 5 months; 1-5 -> 4 months; T >= 5 -> 3 months. The written middle/high ranges overlap at 5. These are source facts, **not a resolved boundary algorithm**.

Eight accepted fixed C5 templates = seven fixed article procedures plus exact-model guide shaft wiping. Eight usage-dependent C5 templates contain 24 published options but no automatically chosen band. The two equipment-dependent articles above are excluded from stock C5 schedules. Pro remains independently limited to its own 1,000-hour guide task. See [profile assessment](PROFILE-RECOMMENDATIONS.md).
