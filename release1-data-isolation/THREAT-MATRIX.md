## Actors, assets and trust boundary

Protect destination printer ownership, original IDs, perpetual rules/baselines, usage, images/notes/currency, exact profile versions, captured owner decisions, logs, immutable observations, current-device acknowledgment and usable full recovery copies. The targeted actor is an **unchanged shipped historical executable**, including a held page or installed app using old assets and its hardcoded `PrintGuard3D` open path. It is uncooperative, not assumed malicious. Future cooperating builds must also be prevented from accidentally writing with an incompatible capability/epoch.

Source snapshots, imported files, profile claims, persisted control values and messages are untrusted inputs to validation. Human cutover acknowledgment is evidence of a decision, not proof that every old client closed. An old source writer is allowed to keep addressing `S`; that residual activity is a divergence risk, not destination authority. A/B must never silently make `D` an alias for `S`.
