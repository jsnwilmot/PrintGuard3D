## Implemented boundary

- A separate, strict, versioned JSON safety reference policy identifies multiple hazard contexts for exact reviewed Creator 5 procedures. The complete existing task/source/model verifier must pass; title keywords, priority, imported claims and adjacent-model similarity never establish a mapping. [SOURCE-REGISTER.csv](SOURCE-REGISTER.csv) records official URLs, retrieval dates, response hashes and scope. [APPLICABILITY.md](APPLICABILITY.md) records mapped and unassessed cases.
- Thermal work is identified from the cold-pull article. Dust/tool/material facts come from individual articles. Electrical/moving-part cautions are clearly labelled **general model notice context**, not verified powered-service sequences. No operating temperatures, blanket unplugging step, repair diagram or technician-only manufacturer restriction is invented. The app does not control hardware. Qualified-assistance advice is application guidance consistent with unchanged disclaimer v1.0, not an exclusive manufacturer requirement.
- Task details and completion show identified references or an explicit unassessed state. Pro's own 1,000-hour task and the general guide wiping task remain unassessed by this limited procedure policy; their source-backed intervals stay intact. Unknown/custom/resin work is never labelled safe. Validated PrintGuard review reminders do not acquire physical service instructions. User Defined modifications cannot use old reminder metadata as a safety-review exemption; they return to the unassessed state.
- Completed work in the first-party form requires the existing general review plus an unchecked contextual acknowledgment for physical/unassessed procedures. The submit handler also checks it. Changing date/outcome resets contextual review. Retrospective wording says **reviewed now for this record**, not before past work; even today's work is not claimed to have been reviewed beforehand. Skipped/not-performed work instead confirms non-performance and asks for no hazardous-work acknowledgment. Offline/flagged sources remain visible and do not prevent truthful past recording after context review.
- The contextual checkbox is **ephemeral, not stored**: it is not evidence of qualifications, a safety certificate, hardware interlock, pre-service review or auditable acknowledgment history. Legacy direct API/cached clients retain their prior behavior. No database, task, log, observation, profile or backup schema changes. See [pre-implementation design](DESIGN.md) and compatibility limits below.
- Concise non-affiliation/brand ownership copy appears in welcome/footer, registration/model selection, printer/task details, profile review, Help and Settings. Names identify equipment only; no logos, registered-mark assertions or legal safe-harbour claims are added. Source labels are explicitly not safety approval; actual suggestions remain review-only.
- Current task explanations carry safety context into reports, notifications and ICS through the existing selector; the existing export safety notice is extended for unknown hazards. CSV/HTML/JSON and print retain notices. Historical logs/observations are not assigned a new past hazard classification or acknowledgment. Accepted manufacturer definitions, intervals, recurrence calculations and historical values stay unchanged.
